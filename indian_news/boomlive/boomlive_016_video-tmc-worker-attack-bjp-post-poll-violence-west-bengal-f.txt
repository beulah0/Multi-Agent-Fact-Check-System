A video showing a youth being brutally assaulted on street in Versova, Mumbai, is being falsely linked to the post poll violence in West Bengal. Several Trinamool Congress leaders shared the video with claims that it shows BJP-backed goons beating a TMC worker post a regime change in Bengal.
BOOM found that the video shows a youth attacked following an argument related to playing cricket and predates the post verdict violence in Bengal.
West Bengal is witnessing multiple incidents of post-poll violence after the BJP secured a sweeping victory, overthrowing the three-time ruling TMC government. On May 6, the aide of Suvendu Adhikari, Chandranath Rath was shot dead in Madhyamgram. He is among at least seven people who were killed after the election verdict in the state.
The Claim
TMC MP Kalyan Banerjee shared the video on X with the caption, "Trinamool Congress workers came under brutal assault allegedly carried out by BJP-backed hooligans. New era!!!!!"
Click here to view the post and here for an archive.
Former journalist-turned-TMC MP Sagarika Ghosh also shared the video with a similar false claim.
What We Found: Video Does Not Show Post-Poll Attack On TMC In Bengal
1. Video Is From Maharashtra: We first broke the video into keyframes and conducted a reverse image search to trace its origin which led us to an Instagram post dated April 15, 2026.
The post stated that the incident took place in Andheri West, Mumbai, Maharashtra. Taking a cue from this, we ran a keyword search in Marathi and came across a news report from April 11, 2026, by local news channel Maharashtra News 9.
According to the report, the victim seen in the video is Mohammed Zaid, and the incident took place in Versova.
We reached out to journalist Nagesh Kalasgaunde, who confirmed that the video shows an assault on a youth in Versova who suffered critical injuries. Kalasgaunde told BOOM, “An FIR was also registered in connection with the incident at Versova Police Station. I also spoke to the victim’s family, who confirmed the same.”
Kalasgaunde shared a copy of the FIR related to the incident.
2. Confirmation From Victim’s Family Friend: We spoke to Zubair Khan, a family friend of the victim, who confirmed that the video shows Mohammed Zaid being assaulted by locals in Mumbai, Maharashtra, on April 9, 2026. Khan told BOOM, “The video was shot in Versova, Mumbai, on April 9, 2026. My friend’s son, Zaid, was beaten up following an argument related to playing cricket. There is no communal or political angle to the incident. We had only shared the video with the journalist after the incident.”
Khan further said that after an FIR was registered at Versova Police Station, the police arrested two people in connection, while one accused remains absconding.
